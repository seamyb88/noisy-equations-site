About

Expert knowledge, explained clearly

Noisy Equations Tutoring offers focused, one-to-one support in maths and physics (although group sessions are available) delivered in person from a fully-kitted clasroom in my home in Newry. Whether your child needs to rebuild confidence with the basics or is aiming for the very top grades, sessions are built around exactly where they are and where they want to be.

With a First Class Honours degree in Physics, an MSc in Cyber Security, a PhD in Applied Mathematics and many years of tutoring experience, I bring a deep understanding of these subjects, and, just as importantly, the ability to break difficult ideas into clear, manageable steps. Every concept has a story behind it, and once students see the "why", the "how" becomes far easier.

I strive to impart the passion I have for these subjects onto my students.

FAQ

Common questions

Where do sessions take place?

All sessions are face-to-face at my home in Newry. Students from Newry and the surrounding areas are very welcome.

Which exam boards do you cover?

I can support students following any of the main exam specifications. Just let me know which board and course your child is studying when you get in touch.

How long is each session?

Sessions last one hour and take place once per week.

How much does tutoring cost?

One-to-one sessions are £40 per session. Group sessions are avaliable, if sufficient demand is there, with a maximum of four students per session at £25 per student per session.
